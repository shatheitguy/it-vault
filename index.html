    💀 DANGER ZONE

    This nukes everything — every asset, contract, employee, ticket, and your branding — back to a blank slate, like the app was just installed. Login accounts are the only thing kept, so you can still get back in. There's no undo button here, only a backup file: one is taken automatically the instant before the wipe runs.

    💀 FACTORY RESET

    This wipes the database completely — every asset, contract, employee, ticket, branding setting and every login account, including your own. The instance returns to the first-run setup wizard, where you create a new admin from scratch. A full backup is saved automatically first, but this can't be undone from here.
